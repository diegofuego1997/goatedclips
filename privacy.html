Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Goated Clips Uploader uses YouTube API Services.

This policy explains how Goated Clips Uploader (“the app”), operated by Goated Clips, handles data. The app is a private, single-user tool used only by Goated Clips to publish its own clips to its own accounts.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): with the account owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and set their title, description and privacy status on the Goated Clips channel, and to read basic channel information needed for uploading.
  • Instagram (Meta): the app uses the Instagram API to publish Reels to the Goated Clips Instagram account and to read basic account information needed for publishing.
  • TikTok: the app uses the TikTok Content Posting API to publish or send videos as drafts to the Goated Clips TikTok account and to read basic profile information required by TikTok for posting.

The app does not access, collect or process data of any other users, viewers or third parties.

How data is used and stored

Access tokens issued by Google, Meta and TikTok are stored only locally on the operator's computer and are used solely to publish Goated Clips content. No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or transferred anywhere other than to the respective platform's official API.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time:

  • Google / YouTube: Google security settings – third-party access
  • Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites in the Instagram app
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions in the TikTok app

When access is revoked, the locally stored tokens become invalid. Local tokens can also be deleted on request.

Data retention and deletion

The app does not keep copies of YouTube, Instagram or TikTok account data. OAuth tokens are kept only as long as the app is in use. To delete all stored data, revoke access via the links above or email goatedclips@proton.me – locally stored tokens are deleted within 7 days of a request.

Third-party services

By using YouTube features of this app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Use of Instagram is subject to Meta's Privacy Policy, and use of TikTok to the TikTok Privacy Policy.
